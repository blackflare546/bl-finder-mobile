# Bluetooth Finder icon

The **Signal Knot** combines two offset signal paths with a precise locator core. The arcs imply scanning and movement without reusing the Bluetooth rune, a magnifying glass, or a literal radar target.

## Color

- Deep navy: `#071B2B`
- Signal teal: `#10A6A0`
- Signal mint: `#6CD6D1`
- Core white: `#FFFFFF`

## Usage

- Use `bluetooth-finder-icon.svg` as the full-bleed master app icon.
- Use `bluetooth-finder-icon-1024.png` as the raster launcher/store master.
- Use `bluetooth-finder-mark.svg` on deep navy or another sufficiently dark background.
- Use `bluetooth-finder-foreground-432.png` as the transparent Android adaptive foreground.
- Use `bluetooth-finder-monochrome.svg` or `bluetooth-finder-monochrome-432.png` for Android themed icons and single-color production.
- Keep clear space around the mark equal to the width of the central diamond.
- Do not add shadows, gradients, glows, outlines, or rotate individual elements.
- Do not place the colored mark directly on teal, mint, or visually noisy imagery.

The symbol is intentionally text-free so it remains recognizable at launcher, notification, and favicon sizes. Trademark clearance has not been performed.
